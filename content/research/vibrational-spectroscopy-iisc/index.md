## Background

Molecular vibrations are quantised. Near equilibrium, each normal mode behaves approximately
as a harmonic oscillator, with energy levels

$$
E_v = \hbar\omega\left(v + \tfrac{1}{2}\right), \qquad v = 0, 1, 2, \dots
$$

and anharmonic corrections show up as overtones and combination bands. Two complementary
probes are:

- **Infrared (IR) absorption:** a mode is IR active when the vibration changes the molecule's
  dipole moment, \(\partial \boldsymbol{\mu} / \partial Q \neq 0\).
- **Raman scattering:** a mode is Raman active when the vibration changes the polarisability,
  \(\partial \alpha / \partial Q \neq 0\).

Peak positions, widths and intensities report on bond strengths, molecular environment and
intermolecular interactions.
